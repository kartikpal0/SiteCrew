Company Profile & Engineering Heritage

A Legacy of Power & Precision

Founded on principles of engineering excellence and statutory safety, Krishna Enterprises has evolved into a premier partner for thermal power utilities, captive co-gens, and grain distilleries across India.

18+ Years of Delivering Uninterrupted Power Generation

Krishna Enterprises was founded to address the critical need for specialized, safety-driven maintenance in high-pressure thermal power environments. Headquartered in Muzaffarnagar (Uttar Pradesh), we have spearheaded operations across multiple states, ensuring plant uptime targets exceeding 99%.

Our corps of over 500 certified engineers, shift in-charges, turbine specialists, and boiler desk controllers operate round-the-clock. We combine traditional metallurgical experience with modern diagnostic tools—including real-time vibration spectrometry, thermographic imaging, and computerized asset maintenance.
