Our 9 Specialized O&M Engineering Services

O&M of Captive & Independent Power Plants (IPP)

We provide end-to-end operational responsibility for captive co-generation and IPP thermal plants. Our certified shift personnel manage the central DCS, field inspections, chemistry controls, and equipment reliability, ensuring availability targets exceeding 99.2%.

Key Deliverables: Continuous 24/7/365 shift rotation, heat-rate minimization, daily chemical logs, water quality testing.
Preventive Protocols: Predictive vibration analysis, lube oil centrifugation, thermal imaging of motor terminals.

AMC & Overhauling of Steam & Gas Turbine Gen Sets

Precision engineering services for high-speed steam and gas turbine generator sets. We perform blade non-destructive testing (NDT), dynamic rotor balancing, casing alignment, and emergency trip governor calibrations.

Turbine Types Serviced: Condensing, Back-pressure, Bleed/Extraction, Combined-Cycle, Multi-stage impulse/reaction sets.

O&M of Ash Handling Plants (AHP)

Specialized operation of fly ash pneumatic conveying and bottom ash wet slurry systems to guarantee zero boiler back-pressure chokes and total environmental compliance with CPCB norms.

Pneumatic Systems: Dense phase fly ash conveying vessels, dome valves, instrument air compressors, and silo bag filters.
Wet Slurry & Dykes: Submerged Scraper Chain Conveyors (SSCC), clinker grinders, high-pressure slurry pipelines.
